Jabali Web brings AI-driven game creation to your browser with a visual experience. Everything you can do on Discord, you can also do on the web, with the added bonus of real-time editing, asset previews and easy content organization.

## Getting started

1. Go to [jabali.ai](https://jabali.ai).
2. Log in with your Google or Discord account.
3. Enter a prompt, or pick one of the suggested prompts for inspiration.
4. Click **Start Generation** to begin your first game.

![The Jabali Web home page with a prompt box and games from the community](../../assets/web/jabali-web.png)

## Work with Bali, Jabali's AI assistant

Bali is your AI game assistant. It walks you through the best way to generate your game and dispatches agents that generate:

- **Core game details:** visual style, writing style, setting and theme
- **Level structure:** level names, environment descriptions and story arcs
- **Characters:** names, personalities, abilities and portraits
- **Assets:** backgrounds, objects, sprites, music and sound effects

![Bali giving feedback on a game idea and suggesting improvements](../../assets/web/bali-chat.png)

Before generation starts, you pick a **genre** for your prompt. Bali recommends genres as you chat.

:::caution[Choose your genre carefully]
Selecting a genre is final. Once you make a choice, it can't be undone.
:::

![Bali asking you to select a genre, with Customize and Vibe Code template options](../../assets/web/bali-genre.png)

## Upload custom content

You can upload your own content to improve generation: a **knowledge file** that enriches the AI's storytelling, or **branded assets** such as your own images and sounds. After you select a genre, Jabali offers you the option to upload content.

![The upload step with optional book or story, music and reference image uploads](../../assets/web/bali-upload.png)

### Supported upload types

| Type               | Description                                                  |
| ------------------ | ------------------------------------------------------------ |
| **Knowledge file** | Text documents used as a knowledge base for AI generation    |
| **Branded asset**  | Custom images or sound files tied to specific in-game assets |

### Supported file types

- **Documents:** `.pdf`, `.txt`, `.doc`, `.docx`
- **Audio:** `.mp3`, `.wav`
- **Images:** `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.gif`

### Limits

- Maximum file size: **5 MB**
- Maximum content length: **1,000 lines**

## Game generation

Once you've chosen a genre, Jabali's agents generate your game from your prompt. You can watch their progress as they work.

![Jabali agents discussing and generating a branching interactive story](../../assets/web/agentic-chatter.png)

When the agents finish, your game is ready to play.

![A finished game, "Masquerade Beneath Marble Skies", with Customize and Play Now buttons](../../assets/web/bali-play.png)

## Customize game content

Click **Customize** to open the game customization window, where you can change:

| Element           | Editable fields                                  |
| ----------------- | ------------------------------------------------ |
| **Game settings** | Visual style, writing style, theme, setting      |
| **Levels**        | Name, description, story synopsis                |
| **Characters**    | Name, description, personality                   |
| **Assets**        | Image previews, prompts                          |

![The game customization window with editable game details, characters, poster and endings](../../assets/web/game-edit.png)

To edit, click any content block and revise it inline. Save your changes and click **Publish**.

:::tip
Playtest early and often. It's the fastest way to refine your game.
:::

## Go further in Jabali Studio

Want templates, Godot and Phaser projects, script editing and version history? Your web games sync to [Jabali Studio](/docs/studio/), the desktop app.
