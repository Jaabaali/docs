New to Jabali? Follow these six stages in order. By the end, you'll have made a game with Bali, changed it, published it and added new features to it, without writing code.

The path follows the six-part cave arcade tutorial series, with the guides you need at each stage. It takes about an hour to read, or about 50 minutes if you watch the tutorial videos instead.

:::tip[Already comfortable with Studio?]
Skip ahead to the [Advanced path](/docs/start/advanced/).
:::

## Stage 1: Get set up

Install Studio and learn the handful of terms the rest of the docs use.

1. [Install Jabali Studio](/docs/studio/install/)
2. [Sign in](/docs/studio/sign-in/)
3. [Key concepts](/docs/studio/concepts/)

**You can now** open Studio and know what Bali, templates, Sparks and publishing mean.

## Stage 2: Make your first game

Turn a short idea into a playable game. The guide covers every way to start; the tutorial shows one start to finish.

1. [Create your first game](/docs/studio/create-a-game/)
2. [Tutorial, part 1: Make your first game](/docs/tutorials/first-game/)

**You can now** turn a prompt into a playable game and playtest it in Studio.

## Stage 3: Change it by asking

Improve your game in plain words, and learn what Bali can and can't do.

1. [Tutorial, part 2: Customize and polish a game](/docs/tutorials/customize-a-game/)
2. [Using Bali](/docs/studio/bali/)

**You can now** change visuals, add an enemy and tune difficulty by asking Bali.

## Stage 4: Share it

Put your game online and keep a safe copy of every version.

1. [Tutorial, part 3: Publish your game](/docs/tutorials/publish-a-game/)
2. [Publish your game](/docs/studio/publish/)
3. [Version history](/docs/studio/version-history/)

**You can now** share a playable link, and roll back a change that broke something.

## Stage 5: Add to it

Grow the game with a new mechanic, new art and your own files.

1. [Tutorial, part 4: Add collectibles and scoring](/docs/tutorials/collectibles-and-scoring/)
2. [Tutorial, part 5: Generate characters and art](/docs/tutorials/characters-and-art/)
3. [Tutorial, part 6: Upload your own art](/docs/tutorials/upload-your-own-art/)
4. [Create assets with Bali](/docs/studio/assets/)

**You can now** add a mechanic with its own art and sound, swap in a new hero, and use files you made yourself.

## Stage 6: Work well with Bali

Pick up the habits that save time and Sparks.

1. [Bali best practices](/docs/studio/bali-best-practices/)
2. [Sparks and usage](/docs/studio/sparks/)

**You can now** size your requests well, report bugs clearly and keep your usage in check.

## Where to go next

- Take on a bigger project with the [Advanced path](/docs/start/advanced/).
- Learn what makes a game feel good in [Design and polish your game](/docs/studio/design-and-polish/).
- Stuck at any point? Check the [FAQ](/docs/studio/faq/) or [get help](/docs/support/) on Discord.
