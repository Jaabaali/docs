After Jabali generates a game, you can **fine-tune every detail directly in Discord**. Whether you're refining the game's tone or rewriting a character's quirks, editing is fast and conversational.

## What you can edit

Jabali supports direct text editing of three key areas:

1. **Core game details**
2. **Level details**
3. **Character profiles**

You can update these with slash commands, by replying to specific bot messages in the channel, or with the **EDIT DATA** button described below.

## Core game details

These settings shape the tone and feel of your whole game.

| Field             | Description                                                                         |
| ----------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------------------------- |
| **Visual style**  | The look and feel of your game (e.g. pixel art, watercolor, 3D flat)                |
| **Writing style** | Narrative tone and dialogue style (e.g. comedic, noir, poetic)                      |
| **Setting**       | The world your game takes place in (e.g. sci-fi colony, haunted forest)             |
| **Theme**         | The underlying message or emotional tone (e.g. redemption, survival, coming of age) |

## How to edit

During generation, look for the **EDIT DATA** button below each generated piece of content. Click it to open a dialog where you can edit the content directly.

## Best practices

- **Keep styles consistent.** If you write in a whimsical tone, apply it across characters and levels.
- **Short is sweet.** Stick to a few sentences per field to keep gameplay snappy.
- **Be descriptive, not vague.** Instead of "cool character", try "a wandering bard with neon tattoos and a cyber-harp."
- **Test your edits** to make sure they carry over to other parts of the game.
