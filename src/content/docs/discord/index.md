Jabali lets you create games directly inside Discord with simple slash commands: no installation, no coding, just you and your imagination. Whether you're a beginner or a pro, Jabali on Discord is the fastest way to start building AI-generated 2D narrative games.

## Step 1: Join the Jabali Discord server

[Join the Jabali Discord server](https://discord.gg/jabali).

## Step 2: Get verified

Once you've joined the server, [get yourself verified](https://discord.com/channels/1203127836844494850/1343882788751015988).
