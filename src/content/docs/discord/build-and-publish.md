## Build your game

Once your game is generated, Jabali shows a **Build and Publish** button. Click it to finalize your game's assets and create a playable build. Building and publishing can take some time.

![The Jabali bot in Discord confirming assets are generated, with a Build and Publish button and a Build Completed message](../../../assets/discord/build-publish.png)

## Rebuild after changes

Any time you change your game, it needs to be rebuilt. Click **Build and Publish** again to rebuild.

## Play your game

When the build is complete, click **Play Game** to play in a desktop or mobile browser. On mobile, Safari on iOS and Chrome on Android are currently supported.

Your game is also playable on the [Jabali website](https://play.production.jabali.ai) and appears on your profile page.

![The Jabali games website showing community games with genre filters](../../../assets/discord/jabali-play-website.jpg)

## Keep building in Jabali Studio

Games you make on Discord sync to [Jabali Studio](/docs/studio/), where you can keep editing them with Bali.
