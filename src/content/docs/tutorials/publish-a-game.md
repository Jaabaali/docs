When you're happy with your game, publish it so anyone with the link can play it.

## Steps

1. [00:00](https://youtu.be/eyTLACis1z4?t=0) Click **Publish** in the top-right corner of Studio.
2. [00:16](https://youtu.be/eyTLACis1z4?t=16) Review the title, description and poster Bali prepared, then click **Publish**.
3. [00:29](https://youtu.be/eyTLACis1z4?t=29) Studio builds the game and gives you a shareable link.
4. [01:39](https://youtu.be/eyTLACis1z4?t=99) Open the link to play the game in a browser, the way players will.

:::note[Current app]
The **Publish Game** dialog also asks for a **Version** (such as `1.0.1`) and optional **Release Notes**. After publishing, click **Copy link** or **Open in browser**. See [Publish your game](/docs/studio/publish/).
:::
