These docs are open source and community-maintained. Anyone can suggest a fix or a new page, and a Jabali maintainer reviews every change before it goes live.

## Fix or improve a page (no setup needed)

1. Click **Edit page** at the bottom of the page you want to change. You'll need a free [GitHub account](https://github.com/signup).
2. GitHub asks you to fork the repository. Click **Fork this repository**.
3. Make your edits in the browser. Pages are written in [Markdown](https://www.markdownguide.org/basic-syntax/).
4. Click **Commit changes…**, describe what you changed, then click **Propose changes**.
5. Click **Create pull request**.

A maintainer will review your change, may suggest edits, and merges it when it's ready. The site updates automatically a few minutes after merge.

## Report a problem instead

Not sure how to fix it? [Open an issue](https://github.com/Jaabaali/docs/issues/new/choose) describing what's wrong or missing.

## Writing guidelines

- **Start with the answer.** Open each page and section with a one- or two-sentence summary of what the reader will learn or do.
- **Use clear, descriptive headings.** Phrase FAQ headings as questions ending in "?".
- **Write steps as numbered lists**, one action per step, with UI labels in **bold**.
- **Add alt text to every image** describing what the screenshot shows.
- **Use product names consistently:** Jabali Studio, Jabali Web, Bali.
- **Only document released features.** Don't describe features that haven't shipped yet.

The full contributor guide, including how to add pages and screenshots and how to preview the site locally, is in [CONTRIBUTING.md on GitHub](https://github.com/Jaabaali/docs/blob/main/CONTRIBUTING.md).
