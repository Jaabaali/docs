# 🌐 Game Generation on the Web

Jabali’s web app gives you the power of AI-driven game creation with a visual experience. Everything you can do in Discord, you can also do on the web — with the added bonus of real-time editing, asset previews, and easy content organization.

---
## 🚀 Getting Started
To begin generating games in your browser:

1. Visit [https://.jabali.ai](https://jabali.ai)  
2. Log in using your Google or Discord account  
3. Enter a Prompt or choose one from the list provided as inspiration.
4. Finally click **"Start Generation"** to begin your first game

![Jabali Web](images/jabali-web.png)

## ✨ Engage with Bali, Jabali's AI Assistant 
Bali is your AI-game assistant and will walk you through the process of identifying the best way to generate your game. Bali can dispatch agents that can generate the following:

![Jabali Web](images/bali-chat.png)

- **🎮 Core Game Details:** Visual style, writing style, setting, theme  
- **🗺️ Level Structure:** Level names, environment descriptions, and story arcs  
- **🧍 Characters:** Names, personalities, abilities, and visual portraits  
- **🎨 Assets:** Backgrounds, objects, sprites, music, and sound effects

But before you do that, you will need to pick a genre for your prompt. As you chat with Bali, it will automatically make genre recommendations. Selecting a genre is final so once you make a choice it cannot be undone. So choose wisely! 

![Jabali Web](images/bali-genre.png)

## 📁 Uploading Custom Content

Jabali lets you upload your own content to enhance game generation — whether it’s a **knowledge file** that enriches the AI’s storytelling, or **branded assets** like custom images and sounds for your game. 

After you slect a genre, you will be presented with the option to upload your content.
![Jabali Web](images/bali-upload.png)

### 🔍 Supported Upload Types

| Type               | Description                                                   |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Knowledge File** | Text documents used as a knowledge base for AI generation     |
| **Branded Asset**  | Custom images or sound files tied to specific in-game assets  |

### 📁 Supported File Types
- Docs: `.pdf`, `.txt`, `.doc` or `.docx` 
- Audio: `.mp3` or `.wav` 
- Music: `.png`, `.jpg`, `.jpeg`, `.gif`

### 📏 Limits
- Max file size: **5MB**
- Max content length: **1000 lines**

## 🧰 Customizing Game Content

On Jabali web you can also choose to customize individual game components by clicking on **Customize**. This will open the game customization window which allows you to change the following:

| Element            | Editable Fields                                                    |
|--------------------|---------------------------------------------------------------------|
| **Game Settings**   | Visual Style, Writing Style, Theme, Setting                        |
| **Levels**          | Name, Description, Story Synopsis                                  |
| **Characters**      | Name, Description, Personality                          |
| **Assets**          | Image Previews, Prompts                |

![alt text](images/game-edit.png)

To edit, simply click on any content block and revise it inline. Save your changes and **Publish** your game.

Playtest early, playtest often — it's the fastest way to refine your game.
